Stay Scale: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026 · Developer: sval01 · Contact: svaldev04@gmail.com

Stay Scale is a puzzle game. This policy explains what data the game uses, why, and the choices you have. We do not ask for your name, email, or any account.

1. Data stored on your device

The game saves your high score and settings (sound, volume, vibration, theme, language, tutorial progress) on your device only. This data is never sent to us. You can delete it with Settings > Reset high score or by uninstalling the game.

2. Advertising (Android version)

The Android version shows ads from Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads (to continue, undo, or double your score) and occasional ads between games. The Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares the following data with Google, encrypted in transit, for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention:

DataExamples
Approximate locationIP address, which may be used to estimate general location
App interactionsApp launches, taps, ad views
DiagnosticsApp launch time, hang rate, energy usage
Device identifiersAndroid advertising ID, app set ID

We (the developer) do not receive this data in a form that identifies you. Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice any time in Settings > Privacy options.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device's Google settings.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional. Declining them does not affect the game.

4. Web version

The web version on itch.io has no ads and the game itself collects no data. itch.io's own privacy policy applies to the website.

5. Children

Stay Scale is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date. Questions or requests: svaldev04@gmail.com.

Kebijakan Privasi Stay Scale

Berlaku: 20 November 2026 · Pengembang: sval01 · Kontak: svaldev04@gmail.com

Stay Scale adalah game puzzle. Game ini tidak meminta nama, email, atau akun apa pun.

1. Data di perangkatmu

Skor tertinggi dan pengaturan (suara, volume, getar, tema, bahasa, tutorial) hanya disimpan di perangkatmu dan tidak pernah dikirim ke kami. Hapus lewat Pengaturan > Hapus skor tertinggi atau dengan mencopot game.

2. Iklan (versi Android)

Versi Android menampilkan iklan Google AdMob. SDK iklan Google otomatis mengumpulkan dan membagikan ke Google, terenkripsi: alamat IP (perkiraan lokasi umum), interaksi aplikasi, data diagnostik, dan ID perangkat (ID iklan Android, app set ID), untuk iklan, analitik, dan pencegahan penipuan. Kami tidak menerima data ini dalam bentuk yang mengidentifikasi kamu.

3. Pilihanmu

  • Di Kawasan Ekonomi Eropa, Inggris, dan Swiss, game meminta persetujuan sebelum iklan yang dipersonalisasi. Ubah kapan saja di Pengaturan > Opsi privasi.
  • ID iklan bisa direset atau dihapus di pengaturan Google perangkatmu.
  • Iklan berhadiah selalu opsional.

4. Versi web

Versi web di itch.io tanpa iklan dan tidak mengumpulkan data.

5. Anak-anak

Stay Scale tidak ditujukan untuk anak di bawah 13 tahun.
